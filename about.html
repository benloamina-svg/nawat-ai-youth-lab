About

From Nucleus to Nation.

NAWAT AI Youth Lab works to make AI literacy and practical digital skills accessible to Algerian youth — so they become makers, not just consumers, of technology.

ABFounder

Meet the founder

Amina Benlouanas

I’m Amina Benlouanas, an Algerian youth leader, educator, and project coordinator with a background in English Civilization and Literature and experience across education, youth empowerment, communication, and organizational development. I have worked with youth-led organizations and initiatives focused on leadership, skills development, culture, and community engagement, while also teaching English and training young people in communication and public speaking.

I’m the founder of Nawat AI Youth Lab, an initiative working to make AI literacy and practical digital skills more accessible to Algerian youth. I’m passionate about creating opportunities where young people can learn, experiment, connect, and turn ideas into real projects.

I’m particularly interested in youth leadership, education, AI, women’s empowerment, international cooperation, and building practical initiatives that create meaningful impact. I believe in learning continuously, starting with what is available, and turning ideas into action.

Who is behind this

NAWAT AI × TIRYAQ NGO

NAWAT AI Youth Lab

A youth-led AI programme based in Biskra, Algeria. Peer trainers run hands-on workshops on ChatGPT, Claude and Google AI Studio.

TIRYAQ NGO — partner

An association for culture and arts in Biskra, running youth initiatives and training on the initiative cycle. TIRYAQ proposed the “From AI Users to AI Makers” workshop.
