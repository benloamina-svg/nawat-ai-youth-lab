Flagship workshop · Proposed for the Pan African Youth Forum 2026

From AI users
to AI makers.

A highly participatory workshop — not a lecture. Participants work individually and in small multicultural groups through short challenges, turning real community problems into AI-supported solutions.

Open the team idea-builder →See the activities

Six short challenges

Less talking. More doing.

The methodology encourages peer learning, cross-country collaboration and knowledge exchange between participants from different African contexts.

  1. 01

    AI Reality Check

    A quick interactive activity exploring how participants currently use AI.

    EXPLORE
  2. 02

    Challenge Mapping

    Identify a real problem affecting young people, education, employment, entrepreneurship or communities.

    DEFINE
  3. 03

    AI Challenge Lab

    Groups experiment with AI tools and prompting techniques to explore possible solutions.

    EXPERIMENT
  4. 04

    Build & Create

    Each group develops a simple solution concept, prototype or action plan.

    MAKE
  5. 05

    African Solutions Showcase

    Groups briefly present their ideas and receive feedback from other participants.

    SHARE
  6. 06

    Commitment to Action

    Each participant identifies one way to apply what they learned after the forum.

    ACT

What participants take away

More than new knowledge.

A solution concept

An initial AI-supported response to a real challenge.

New connections

Young innovators from different countries and backgrounds.

A next step
