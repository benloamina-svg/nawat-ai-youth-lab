The problem

Youth groups have the energy. They lack the structure.

Same activities, again

Small, volunteer-run NGOs and clubs repeat the same few formats because designing something new feels hard.

Ideas that ignore resources

Plans assume a budget, a venue and partners. Most youth groups start with a room, some phones and motivated volunteers.

AI used only to “ask for answers”

Members copy answers from AI instead of using it as a thinking partner to plan, write and evaluate their projects.

Insight from NAWAT AI and TIRYAQ NGO field work with youth in Biskra, Algeria: young people rarely lack motivation — they lack a simple path from problem to plan.

Example idea

Volunteer Passport

Young volunteers collect a stamp and a short reflection after every activity. After six weeks they hold a verifiable record of their skills — and a reason to come back.
