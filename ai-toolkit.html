Use AI as a thinking partner, not an answer machine.

What AI is good — and bad — at

Good for

  • Brainstorming many ideas fast
  • Turning rough notes into a clear plan
  • Drafting posts, emails and concept notes
  • Designing surveys and simple evaluations
  • Translating and simplifying texts

Be careful with

  • Facts, numbers and laws — AI can invent them
  • Local context it does not know
  • Personal or sensitive data — never paste it
  • Bias: who is missing from the answer?
  • Final decisions — they stay human

The prompt formula

Role + Context + Task + Format + Constraints

Role

“Act as a youth programme coordinator…”

Context

Who you are, your community, your resources.

Task

Exactly what you want it to produce.

Format & constraints

Table, 100 words, simple English, no budget…

Prompt library

Ready-to-copy prompts for NGO work

Replace the parts in [brackets]. Want prompts filled in automatically? Play the Initiative Idea Game.

Plan an initiative

Act as an experienced youth programme coordinator. Our [NGO / club] in [city, country] wants to address [problem] for [target group]. We have [resources]. Suggest 3 creative initiative ideas, then turn the best one into a 4-week action plan with tasks, owners and a simple budget. Use a table.

Write a funding concept note

Act as a grant writer for youth NGOs. Write a one-page concept note for [initiative name], which addresses [problem] for [target group] in [place]. Include: context, objectives, activities, expected results, budget summary of [amount], and sustainability. Formal but simple English. Ask me for any missing information first.

Create social media posts

Act as a social media manager. Write 3 Instagram posts to promote [initiative] for [target group]: one announcement, one reminder, one thank-you after the event. Each under 100 words, with a call to action and 4 hashtags. Tone: [warm / energetic / professional].

Design a survey

Act as a monitoring and evaluation officer. Create an anonymous survey (max 8 questions) to understand [topic] among [target group]. Mix multiple choice, a 1–5 scale and one open question. Explain what each question helps us learn.

Summarise a meeting

Act as an NGO secretary. Summarise these meeting notes into: decisions taken, action points (who / what / deadline), and open questions. Keep it under 200 words. Notes: [paste your notes — remove names and personal data first].

Translate materials

Act as a professional translator for youth NGOs. Translate the text below into [Arabic / French / English] for [audience]. Keep the meaning, use natural local wording, and list any words that may need a cultural check. Text: [paste text].

Responsible use

Build with AI. Think beyond AI.

  • Check facts and numbers before you publish.
  • Never paste names, phone numbers or private data of participants.
  • Look for bias and ask who may be excluded.
  • Say when content was made with AI help.
  • Keep people, context and human judgement at the centre.
