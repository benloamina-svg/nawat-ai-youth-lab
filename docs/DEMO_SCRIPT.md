# Demo video script — 3 minutes

Record your screen with the live site open (laptop, full screen, Chrome or Edge). Speak calmly; it's fine to read. Free tools: **Clipchamp** (built into Windows), **OBS Studio**, or **Loom**.
Before recording: clear the canvas (Plan it → Clear) so the demo starts fresh, and zoom the browser to 110%.

| Time | On screen | What you say |
|---|---|---|
| **0:00–0:25** · Problem | Home page, scroll slowly through "The problem" | "Hi, I'm Amina Benlouanas, founder of NAWAT AI Youth Lab in Biskra, Algeria. Working with youth NGOs and clubs, I kept seeing the same thing: young people have energy, but no structure. They repeat the same activities, plan with budgets they don't have, and use AI only to copy answers." |
| **0:25–0:40** · Solution | Back to top, hover the constellation | "NAWAT AI Youth Lab is a free website that turns 'we want to do something' into a real initiative — and teaches AI by applying it to your own project. No account, works on any phone." |
| **0:40–1:30** · Idea Game | Click **Play the Idea Game**. Pick: Climate & environment → Rural youth → Teamwork → click a suggested problem → Campaign → Club → Nothing yet → **Generate** | "I make seven choices: theme, who it's for, the skill to build, the problem, the format, my type of group, and what I actually have — here, nothing yet. In seconds I get a named initiative with an activity plan, team roles, success indicators and a zero-budget version." Click **Shuffle**: "Shuffle gives a different creative angle." |
| **1:30–1:50** · AI prompts | Scroll to "Develop it with AI", click **Copy prompt** | "Each idea comes with three ready prompts — a project plan, a promo post and a feedback survey — pre-filled with my choices. Users learn good prompting by using it on their own project, in ChatGPT, Claude or Google AI Studio." |
| **1:50–2:25** · Plan it | Click **Plan it on the Initiative Canvas** → canvas fills. Scroll to SMART Court: select S, M, T for "Empower youth in Biskra" → **Give my verdict** | "One click moves the idea onto our 13-block Initiative Canvas, based on TIRYAQ NGO's training worksheets. Then the SMART Goals game: is 'Empower youth in Biskra' a good goal? No — it's not specific, measurable or time-bound. Here's the SMART version." |
| **2:25–2:40** · Workshop | Workshops page → scroll the six activities → idea-builder → **Download as image** | "For our workshop 'From AI Users to AI Makers', teams fill this builder on their phones, download their idea card and submit it to the showcase." |
| **2:40–3:00** · Impact | About page / home "Built to keep going" | "It's open source, costs nothing to run, and will power NAWAT AI and TIRYAQ workshops from next month — with Arabic and French versions next. Don't just use AI. Make something meaningful with it. Thank you!" |
